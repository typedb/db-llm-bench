Question: Counting the events directly contained by the pathway with stable identifier R-HSA-168256 as depth 1, how many events below that pathway are reachable at two or more different depths?
```
MATCH path = (:Pathway {stId:'R-HSA-168256'})-[:hasEvent*]->(e)
WITH e, count(DISTINCT length(path)) AS depths
WHERE depths > 1
RETURN count(*)
```
