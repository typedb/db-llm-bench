Question: Across Homo sapiens reaction-like events, how many pairings of an event with one of its compartments are recorded? Count each event-and-compartment pairing separately, so an event assigned three compartments contributes three pairings.
```
match
  $r isa reaction-like-event;
  species-assignment (classified-thing: $r, species: $sp); $sp has display-name "Homo sapiens";
  compartment-assignment (localised-thing: $r, compartment: $c);
select $r, $c;
distinct;
reduce $count = count;
```
